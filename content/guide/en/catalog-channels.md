# Blogs, interviews, and research updates

Use technical blogs for explanations, original releases for what a team reports, and interviews for experience and perspectives. A news report or interview can suggest a question; trace technical claims to the paper, documentation, or underlying evaluation. Specific readings appear in [author blogs](blogs.md) and [LessWrong selections](lesswrong.md); [labs](labs.md) introduces research teams.

## Technical blogs and discussion

- **[Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/)** — A practical blog in English documenting experiments with tools and how they behave. Follow the examples and results to see how he assesses their usefulness.

- **[Sebastian Raschka: homepage and articles](https://sebastianraschka.com/)** — English technical articles and implementations on LLM architectures, training, and evaluation, with projects that build models from scratch.

- **[Karpathy: homepage](https://karpathy.ai/)** — Courses, blogs, projects, and social accounts from the author. [X / Twitter](https://twitter.com/karpathy)

- **[LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/about)** — A community discussing research thinking, AI, and rationality. Specific essays are on the LessWrong selections page.

## Research releases and media

- **[Jiqizhixin](https://www.jiqizhixin.com/)** — Chinese technology coverage of papers, projects, and industry. Some material requires a subscription.

- **[OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/)** — Original model, research, and product releases from OpenAI.

- **[Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research)** — Original research releases, browsable by topic.

## Embodied-AI perspectives

- **[Huazhe Xu: Looking Back at Embodied AI in 2025](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1983661736180589668)** — A year-in-review essay in Chinese, linked from Lumina's year-in-review section.

- **[Tianwei Lin: VLA in 2025—the Distance from Demos to Generality](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1989799567177307432)** — A year-in-review essay in Chinese on embodied VLA, linked from Lumina's year-in-review section.

- **[Interview with Jianming Wang: Data as a Core Robotics Problem](https://www.leiphone.com/category/ai/SJMi7xhrknuVadzq.html)** — A Chinese interview about robot data, sharing the interviewee's observations about industry and research.

## Interviews and industry commentary

- **[Z Potentials](https://zpotentials.substack.com/)** — Interviews with AI teams, founders, and technical practitioners about research, products, and startup decisions.

- **[Funeral AI](https://funeralai.substack.com/)** — Chinese writing on AI products and industry developments, with a direct, personal voice.

- **[Xiaojun Zhang: Business Interviews](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/podcast/626b46ea9cbbf0451cf5a962)** — Long-form Chinese conversations with researchers and founders about technology, careers, and important choices.

- **[WhyNotTV](https://space.bilibili.com/14145636/)** — Chinese researcher interviews and personal accounts of university and research life. See [Lookout](lookout.md) for recommended episodes.
