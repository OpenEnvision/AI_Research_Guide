## Courses and textbooks: build a connected foundation

The [course page](courses.md) distinguishes MOOC platforms, university courses, open books, and official practical tutorials. It identifies language, prerequisites, and suggested outcomes by topic. The [foundation catalog](catalog-foundation.md) and [direction catalog](catalog-directions.md) remain available for locating material around a specific question.

Check the syllabus, readings, assignments, offering, and public-access scope. Videos, downloadable notes, graded assignments, and credentials may have different access conditions. An accessible landing page does not imply that all materials are free. Older courses can remain valuable for foundations; pair their implementation details with documentation for the version you use.

[Deep-learning courses and paper readings](limu.md) connects Dive into Deep Learning with detailed paper discussions. Implement and complete exercises alongside videos; viewing progress alone does not establish understanding.

## Practice and projects: distinguish the objective

The [practice page](practice.md) groups Python and algorithms, SQL, mathematics and ML, data competitions, and engineering exercises. Algorithm problems develop bounded problem-solving skills; assignments connect concepts to implementation; competitions compare solutions under specified rules. Research additionally requires question selection, evidence design, and interpretation of limitations.

For each exercise, retain the approach, complexity or compute budget, failure analysis, and subsequent validation. Use reference solutions and AI assistance as feedback after attempting an independent explanation. Follow course and competition rules on collaboration, external data, and automated tools.

## Assemble one complete learning unit

Choose one row for your starting point. These are guide-authored combinations, not a requirement to start every route. A main course provides structure, exercises expose gaps, and projects with reports test transfer.

| Starting point or goal | A useful combination | Output and review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Python and programming | CS50P or BIT Python + one Exercism/LeetCode topic + a local data tool | Validation, tests, complexity, and run instructions |
| Classical ML | ISLP or scikit-learn MOOC + tabular classification/regression | Leakage-free pipelines, baselines, validation selection, and independent testing |
| Deep learning | D2L or a DL main course + a small training project | Gradient/state checks, curves, checkpoints, and failures |
| Specialist research | One topic course + directly relevant original papers + a controlled reproduction | Literature matrix, hypothesis, matched conditions, and limits |
| Engineering or trustworthiness | A fixed baseline + one engineering/responsible-AI experiment | Reproducible settings, condition-specific results, limitations, and maintenance records |

Find original entries on the [course](courses.md) and [practice](practice.md) pages, then connect them through [specialized topics](advanced-topics.md), [worked experimental designs](research-methods.md#worked-prediction), [engineering](engineering.md), and [responsible AI](responsible-ai.md).

## Data, evaluation, and research materials

[Datasets and evaluation](datasets.md) links official resources across tabular data, vision, language, speech, graphs, retrieval, agents, robotics, and systems. Establish the target setting, permissions, sampling unit, splits, and primary metric before selecting an implementation.

Use [research methods](research-methods.md) and the [research tool catalog](catalog-research.md) to organize experiments, diagnose training, control comparisons, record variability, and prepare artifacts. Models, software, code, and data can have different versions and permissions; record them separately.

## Selection and revision standards

Official entry points for newly added courses, practice platforms, and data resources were checked during this revision on **2026-10-16**. This does not mean that every historical link, assignment, or paid resource was individually accessed. Suggested deliverables are editorial recommendations, not provider promises.

Check provenance, resource type, language, prerequisites, publication date, version, and access conditions. Distinguish established explanations, experiential advice, and claims with limited evidence. Report broken links, ambiguous terminology, or bilingual inconsistencies through the [guide repository](https://github.com/OpenEnvision/ai_research_guide), with the page, issue, and proposed source.
