Learning & research

How do you move from learning AI to doing research?

  • Learn Connect mathematics, programming, and core ideas in machine learning.
  • Read Understand a paper’s question, methods, and evidence.
  • Test Frame a clear question; test ideas through experiments or analysis.

Develop your own judgment through study, reading, and practice.
